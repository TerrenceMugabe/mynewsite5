Work / 01

Turning a complex gas installation purchase into a short run of easy decisions.

A configurator for gas geysers, hobs, Rent-to-Own and combo installs, with live pricing. I worked on how customers decide, and on the logic behind it.

4customer flows reworked: combo, geysers, Rent-to-Own and the cost estimator
Where
The Gas Company
Type
UX, pricing logic, conversion

The problem

Customers choosing a geyser, hob or both faced a long path: hobs hidden inside 'kits', a toggle to show or hide models, and the same choice asked twice. The complexity was real (internal and external installs, cylinder sizes, cages, weather covers, granite cutting, Rent-to-Own, combos), so the flow had to carry it without showing it all at once.

What I built

BeforeAfter
Pick a hob "kit"Pick the hob itself, after the geyser and its options
Show or hide all geyser modelsOnly geysers that fit the customer's three quick-question answers
Open "your rent-to-own deals", then browseChoose hobs, geysers or a bundle, then see cards with appliance, monthly price and contract inclusions
Estimator quote possible with blanksNumbered, adaptive steps; quote locked until every needed choice is made

How it works

  • Price at the point of choice. The total updates beside the decision that changes it.
  • Never ask twice. Known answers are never asked again.
  • Every step earns its place. If it doesn't make the decision easier, it goes.
